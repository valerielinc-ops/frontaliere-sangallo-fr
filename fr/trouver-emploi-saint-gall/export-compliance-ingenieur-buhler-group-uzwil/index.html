Entreprise et contexte

  • As part of our Trade Affairs organization, you will help ensure that cutting-edge technologies can be successfully and compliantly deployed around the world. Aufgaben:
  • You evaluate products, technologies, software, and technical data to determine export control classifications (ECN/ECCN) and licensing requirements
  • You review technical drawings, engineering documentation, product specifications, and technology transfers to ensure compliance with international export control regulations
  • You perform screenings, risk assessments, and end-use/end-user evaluations while supporting global customer projects from offer to delivery
  • You maintain export control records, classifications, licenses, and audit-ready documentation to ensure full regulatory compliance and traceability
  • You collaborate with engineering, project management, supply chain, and sales teams while driving process improvements, digitalization initiatives, and stakeholder training Anforderungen:
  • University degree in a technical discipline such as Electrical Engineering, Electronics Engineering, Automation Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, or a comparable field; additional knowledge of international trade or compliance is a plus
  • Strong analytical skills with the ability to understand and assess technical products, systems, drawings, and documentation
  • Independent, structured, and detail- and solution-oriented working style combined with strong cross-functional problem-solving capabilities
  • High level of responsibility, reliability, and integrity when dealing with complex technical and regulatory topics; experience with export control regulations or SAP GTS is advantageous
