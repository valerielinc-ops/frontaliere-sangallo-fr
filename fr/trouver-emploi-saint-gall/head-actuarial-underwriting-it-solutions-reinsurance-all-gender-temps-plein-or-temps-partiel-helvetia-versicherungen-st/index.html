Description complète

IT / Computer Science Workload: 50% - 100% Place of work: Level of experience: Management FlexOffice: on-site Anyone taking out insurance wants to feel secure. You can assist our customers here – through risk transfer and capital optimization in numerous risk classes. Based on our Swiss values, our vision is to be a strong reinsurance partner throughout the world, both in life and in non-life insurance. Are you on board – and ready to be part of our dynamic, sustainable growth?

What you'll be doing

  • Lead and develop a small team of solution engineers and developers, building the capabilities needed to deliver actuarial and underwriting IT solutions for Active Reinsurance
  • Take end-to-end accountability for the UW & Actuarial application portfolio, ensuring stable operations as well as continuous development of our in-house built solutions
  • Drive high-quality and predictable delivery, from requirements and solution design through development, testing, deployment and production support
  • Manage IT costs, balancing business value, service needs and the sustainable development of the IT landscape
  • Take ownership of applications and IT services supporting actuarial and underwriting activities, ensuring they evolve in line with business requirements
  • Shape the Active Reinsurance IT strategy and target landscape, connecting technology direction with actuarial and underwriting priorities
  • Act as a trusted technology partner for the ARI business, translating business needs into clear technology priorities and solutions, explain best practices and build buy-in for solutions and improvements
  • Collaborate across Architecture, Data, Security and other IT teams, as well as external partners, to deliver integrated and sustainable solutions

What you'll bring to the role

  • Experience leading and developing solution engineers and developers, with the ability to build technical capability and support team growth.
  • A leadership style characterized by ownership, transparency, collaboration and a strong delivery mindset, combined with sound decision-making
  • Experience in insurance and reinsurance environments is a strong advantage, complemented by very good English
  • German is an advantage
  • Experience in IT application service ownership, combining commercial awareness with accountability for the solutions in your responsibility
  • The ability to shape IT strategy and target landscapes, connecting longer-term direction with the development of existing tools and applications
  • Strong stakeholder management and communication skills, enabling you to explain technical best practices, collaborate with business teams and build support for change
