TSMG recrute Depot Specialist (Robotaxi Fleet Operations) pour des activites terrain a St. Gallen. Ci-dessous tu trouves la description complete Lever avec responsabilites, exigences et details pratiques du projet. About the Role We are looking for Depot Specialists to support the daily operations of autonomous vehicle (AV) and robotaxi fleets across Europe. As a Depot Specialist, you will play a key role in ensuring autonomous vehicles are clean, fully charged, inspected, and operationally ready for daily deployment. Working from a dedicated robotaxi depot, you will support vehicle preparation, fleet readiness, inventory management, and day-to-day depot operations that enable safe and reliable autonomous ride-hailing services. This position is ideal for candidates who enjoy hands-on work, are highly organized, and thrive in fast-paced operational environments. No previous experience with autonomous vehicles is required - training will be provided. By joining our team, you will contribute directly to the successful launch, operation, and scaling of robotaxi fleets and autonomous mobility services across multiple European cities. ## Vue d'ensemble du projet ## Key

  • Responsibilities
  • Prepare autonomous vehicles for daily robotaxi fleet operations.
  • Ensure every vehicle is cleaned, inspected, charged, and deployment-ready before the start of each shift.
  • Verify the cleanliness and condition of cameras, LiDAR, radar, mirrors, sensors, windows, and vehicle interiors to support optimal self-driving system performance.
  • Perform pre-deployment and post-shift inspections following established operational procedures.
  • Monitor battery levels and charging schedules for electric autonomous vehicles.
  • Support daily robotaxi depot operations by coordinating vehicle movement, parking, charging, and staging.
  • Assist with vehicle dispatch and return processes.
  • Organize parking areas to maximize operational efficiency.
  • Maintain depot cleanliness and ensure work areas comply with safety standards.
  • Coordinate vehicle availability with Safety Operators, Technicians, and Operations Leads.
  • Perform basic vehicle functionality checks before deployment.
  • Verify tire condition, lighting, fluid levels, charging equipment, and general vehicle condition.
  • Identify cosmetic damage or operational issues requiring technical inspection.
  • Escalate maintenance concerns to Senior Technicians or SDS Specialists.
  • Support vehicle recovery and transportation activities when required.
  • Maintain inventory of cleaning materials, charging accessories, PPE, and operational equipment.
  • Track inventory usage and report replenishment needs.
  • Receive deliveries and organize depot storage areas.
  • Assist with logistics supporting fleet expansion and new depot launches.
  • Complete digital inspection checklists and fleet readiness reports.
  • Record vehicle status, charging information, and operational observations.
  • Follow depot safety procedures and company operational standards.
  • Support continuous improvement initiatives that increase fleet availability, reduce downtime, and improve operational efficiency. ##

  • Good organizational and time management skills.
  • Strong attention to detail.
  • Basic computer literacy.

  • Comfortable working in indoor and outdoor environments.

  • Upper-Intermediate English.

  • Experience supporting 24/7 operational environments.

You enjoy structured work, have excellent attention to detail, and understand the importance of ensuring every autonomous vehicle leaves the depot clean, charged, safe, and fully prepared for customer service or testing operations. No previous robotaxi or autonomous vehicle experience is required
