Postdoc position in placenta-embryo-on-chip model for safety assessment

The Nanomaterials in Health Lab is an interdisciplinary research group dedicated to understanding thow particulate materials interact with human cells, tissues and biological barriers. By combining advanced human-relevant models, toxicology and materials science, we contribute to understanding particle-biology interactions and developing robust approaches for safety assessment and biomedical applications of particluate materials.

This postdoctoral project will advance a microfluidic placenta–embryo model, from a research prototype towards a standardised test platform for safety assessment during pregnancy. By integrating placental barrier functions with readouts of early embryonic development, the platform aims to capture effects that may be overlooked when these systems are studied separately. The project combines advanced cell culture, organ-on-chip technology and toxicology to establish reproducible experimental procedures, evaluate model performance and define potential applications in the safety assessment of chemicals, pharmaceuticals and novel materials.

Your tasks

  • Optimise and standardise a microfluidic placenta–embryo model for human-relevant safety assessment
  • Establish biological and toxicological readouts of placental function and early embryonic development
  • Assess assay reproducibility and robustness, and define quality criteria
  • Evaluate model performance using reference substances and comparisons with established methods and available human data
  • Publish and present research findings and collaborate with interdisciplinary project partners

Your profile

  • A PhD in biology, toxicology, biomedical engineering or a related field
  • High motivation to tackle an interdisciplinary challenge at the interface of biology, engineering and toxicology
  • Extensive hands-on experience with advanced mammalian cell culture and in vitro assays
  • Expertise in microphysiological platforms, stem-cell based models or biological barrier models is highly beneficial
  • A rigorous, independent working approach and strong analytical and collaborative skills
  • Excellent communication skills and fluency in English, both spoken and written

Our offer

  • A highly stimulating research environment in the Empa laboratories in St.Gallen
  • Support from experienced research teams
  • Access to state-of-the-art infractructure and equipment
  • Opportunity for close collaboration and networking with experts from different fields

The position is available from February 2027 or later and limited to 2 years. The candidate will perform research at Empa in St. Gallen in collaboration with ETH Zürich ( D-BSSE ) and the Swiss Center for Applied Human Toxicology ( SCAHT ).
